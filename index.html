Ratings from RateMyProfessors, matched per professor AND course. "Easiness proxy" maps RMP difficulty 1-5 to Layup List's -1..+1 vote units; it is a linear proxy, not a measured Dartmouth score. Sections without a matching professor-course pair show no rating. Every rating shows its sample size (n); single-review figures are shown, not hidden.
